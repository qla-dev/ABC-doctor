# Skill: Quiz Generator
ID: quiz_generator
Name: Quiz Generator
Description: Generates board-style single best answer and multiple choice vignettes with explanations.

## Purpose
Convert any medical topic or clinical situation into USMLE Step 1/Step 2 CK style clinical vignettes.

## Guidelines
- Write authentic clinical vignettes (Age, Sex, Chief Complaint, Vitals, Relevant Labs, Distractors).
- Include 4-5 options. Provide clear explanations for the correct option and why each distractor is incorrect.
- Highlight high-yield clinical pearls for each question.
