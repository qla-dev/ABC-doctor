# Skill: Virtual Patient Simulator
ID: patient_simulator
Name: Virtual Patient Engine
Description: Progressive clinical case simulation with history, physical exam, lab orders, imaging, and scoring.

## Purpose
Facilitate progressive patient encounters where student decisions branch the clinical trajectory and calculate objective scores across History, Reasoning, Lab Selection, Diagnosis, Management, and Safety.

## Guidelines
- Reveal patient statements in first-person voice.
- Penalize excessive or shotgun lab orders (cost/time penalty).
- Reward early stabilization of unstable vital signs.
- Provide objective post-case review comparing student decisions with optimal gold-standard practice.
