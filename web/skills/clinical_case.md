# Skill: Clinical Case Generator
ID: clinical_case
Name: Clinical Case Simulation
Description: Interactive clinical problem solver testing diagnostic trajectory and management.

## Purpose
Simulate realistic bedside clinical encounters with progressive disclosure of symptoms, vitals, lab test outcomes, and treatment responses.

## Guidelines
- Start with a realistic patient triage summary.
- Challenge the student to formulate differential diagnoses before revealing labs.
- Point out high-risk "can't-miss" diagnoses (e.g., Aortic Dissection, Tension Pneumothorax, Necrotizing Fasciitis).
